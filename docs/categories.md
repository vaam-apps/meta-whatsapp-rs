# Meta platform categories: what we implement

The areas of Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, grouped as Meta groups
its docs (the directory tree of its documentation), and what
meta-whatsapp-rs implements in each: in the library, and in the service
(`meta-whatsapp-server`).
Verified against `main` at b6fc893 (PR #20) on 2026-09-26. The
per-feature detail is in [coverage.md](coverage.md) (the linked rows);
the capability-level comparison with Zaileys is in [parity.md](parity.md)
(the rows named); the plan is [roadmap.md](roadmap.md).

## Where we stand

| Library | 19 | 17 | 4 |
| Service | 4 | 21 | 15 |

35 categories follow Meta's grouping, 2 are APIs Meta documents
by reference only (36, 37), and 3 are our own (38–40).

## How to read the table

- **Status** is `library / service`: **done**; **partial**, when a
  documented part of the category is missing (the notes say which) or
  the service carries only part of it; **gap**, when nothing is built
  yet. The service's milestone is in parentheses
  ([roadmap.md](roadmap.md)).
- **Meta docs area**: paths relative to
  `https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/`;
  **(nm)** marks a page the mirror cannot fetch, as in
  [parity.md](parity.md#how-to-read-the-table).
- **Coverage rows** link to [coverage.md](coverage.md); every one of its
  rows 1–33 appears at least once.

## How to keep this true

A pull request that moves a category's status updates its row here in
the same PR, with its parity rows and coverage rows (see
[parity.md § How to keep this true](parity.md#how-to-keep-this-true)).
A new area Meta adds to its docs gets the next free number.
